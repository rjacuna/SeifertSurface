The starting surface

One disk per strand, stacked along the braid axis, one half-twisted band per crossing: the Bennequin surface of the closed braid. Its boundary is the wire. The examples ship with their film already computed; anything else is tamed and settled here, and so is any example once a setting below is changed. Untick tame on build to see the scaffold instead, and Rebuild always computes it here.

Taming the wire

Scharein's KnotPlot forces, as van Wijk and Cohen use them: each point of the wire is pulled by its two neighbours (F_a = H\,r^{1+\beta}) and pushed by every other point (F_r = K\,r^{-(2+\alpha)}), damped, and a move that would bring a strand within reach of another is refused, so the knot type cannot change. The film follows. The wire grows while it relaxes.

The film

The surface shown is always the soap film on the wire, kept settled: implicit mean curvature flow with a growing step, then the harmonic step (Pinkall–Polthier), remeshing and tangential smoothing between rounds. It starts again whenever the wire moves.

Display

Soap film

Thin-film interference from a per-vertex thickness in nanometres (thick at the bottom and at the wire, thin at the top, gently uneven): the colour of a free water film of that thickness, computed over the visible spectrum (Newton's series); the film goes black where it is thinnest.
