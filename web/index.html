The starting surface

One disk per strand, stacked along the braid axis, one half-twisted band per crossing: the Bennequin surface of the closed braid. Its boundary is the wire, which never moves again.

Relaxation

Each round freezes the metric of the mesh and solves the Laplace equation \Delta x = 0 for the interior vertices with the wire as boundary data (Pinkall–Polthier); with a finite step it is implicit mean curvature flow. The area decreases every round; the fixed point is the discrete minimal surface.
